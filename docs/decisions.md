| D-25 | 2026-11-04 | Spotify: saved playlists, liked songs, and starting Spotify (owner request). (1) Two more permissions are asked at sign-in, `playlist-read-private` and `playlist-read-collaborative`: JARVIS lists the **names and addresses** of the person's playlists (up to 200) to find "my workout playlist". It never reads what is inside a playlist, the saved songs, the email address or the listening history, and it can change nothing. (2) On macOS, "play <song>", "play my ... playlist" and "play my liked songs" now **start the Spotify app when it is closed** (`/usr/bin/open -g -b com.spotify.client`: a fixed bundle id, in the background, no dialog if it is not installed; nothing from the person, the model or Spotify reaches the command), then wait up to 12 s for it to take the song. (3) A plain "play <words>" is sorted by what Spotify's search returns: the whole request equal to an artist's name plays that artist; a short request, or one a song's title and artist cover at least half of, plays that song; a request ending in a generic word ("mix", "vibes", "hits", "music", "songs") or describing a mood that matches no song well plays the best matching playlist. (4) **A song plays on its own (macOS).** The Spotify app plays a track inside its album by default, so the album's next song followed.
